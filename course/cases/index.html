Secure AI Deployment · companion to Module 03

Case library and attack atlas

Fifty-four documented cases from 2023 to 2026 in which an agent’s tools, credentials, approvals or tool definitions did, or could have done, the damage, then the 23 attack classes they fall into and the 37 controls that bound, reduce or detect them. Everything is described by mechanism (what text got in, what call resulted, whose authority it used) and mapped to the layer of the Module 03 lab that would have bounded it.

How to read this. Not every case is a breach: each says whether it rests on a vendor advisory, a finder’s write-up, an operator’s report or press. Every case was checked on 24 October 2026. Verified means a primary source was read: the finder’s own write-up, a vendor advisory, a CVE record or the operator’s report. Corroborated means the original statement could not be read but independent outlets agree. Partial means only secondary reports were readable and a material detail is missing, which the entry states. A finding that rests on one researcher’s account says so. No payload text or exploit commands are reproduced. Source pages are third-party material and open in a new tab.

    What bounds it

    Thirty-seven controls in eight families (the three testing controls share one row, so 35 rows), each sorted as a boundary, a reducer or a detector, with the single condition that turns it into a real boundary. The sorting is this course’s judgement, built from the standards and vendor guidance cited elsewhere on this site, and it is the question to ask of every control you are sold: what would make this a boundary?
