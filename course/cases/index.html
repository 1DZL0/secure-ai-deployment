Agent tool-misuse case library

Fifty-four documented cases from 2023 to 2026 in which an agent’s tools, credentials, approvals or tool definitions did, or could have done, the damage. Some were breaches, many are vulnerabilities disclosed by researchers, and some are researcher demonstrations. Each is described by mechanism (what text got in, what call resulted, whose authority it used) and mapped to the layer of the Module 03 lab that would have bounded it.

How to read this. Not every entry is a breach: each says whether it rests on a vendor advisory, a finder’s write-up, an operator’s report or press. Every entry was checked on 24 October 2026 against at least one primary or near-primary source: the finder’s own write-up, a vendor advisory, a CVE record or the operator’s report. Verified means a primary source was read. Corroborated means the original statement could not be read but independent outlets agree. Partial means only secondary reports were readable and a material detail is missing, which the entry states. A finding that rests on one researcher’s account says so. No payload text or exploit commands are reproduced. Source pages are third-party material and open in a new tab.
